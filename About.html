About StudentHub

StudentHub is a simple university information portal created to help students explore academic information, departments, and campus resources. This project is designed as part of a web development learning journey using HTML5.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide students with a simple and organized platform where they can easily access important university information and learning resources.

Our Vision

Our vision is to create a user-friendly student portal that supports learning, improves accessibility to information, and enhances the overall student experience.

Why Choose StudentHub?

  • Easy-to-use interface
  • Well-organized information
  • Student-friendly design
  • Quick access to important resources

Our Goals

StudentHub aims to organize university-related information in one place while helping beginner web developers practice HTML through a real-world project.

Our Inspiration
